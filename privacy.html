Splat Siege Privacy Policy

Veyrox Games · Effective 27 September 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Splat Siege (package com.splatsiege.game), made by Veyrox Games ("we"), collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: veyroxgame@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhyWhen
Random player ID and session tokenIdentify your game profile, keep you signed inAlways (created automatically, no personal details)
Player name you chooseShown to opponents and on the leaderboardAlways
Username and passwordLet you restore progress on another phoneOnly if you create an account. Passwords are handled by Unity Authentication; we never see them.
Game progress (trophies, cards, chests, coins, gems, quests, Paint Pass)Save your progress and prevent cheatingAlways
Purchase records (order id, product bought)Deliver in-app purchases and prevent fraud. Payment details are handled by Google Play; we never see them.Only if you buy something in the shop
Leaderboard entry (player name, trophies)Show rankingsAlways
IP address (temporary)Connect you to online matchesDuring matchmaking and online matches
Gameplay statistics (match results, cards used, paint coverage), device model, OS and app version, countryBalance and improve the gameOnly if you tap "Allow" on the analytics question. You can turn it off anytime in Settings.

We do not collect your real name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or payment card details. The game currently shows no ads.

Who processes the data

The game uses Unity Gaming Services by Unity Technologies (Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Leaderboards, Lobby, Relay, Analytics) to store and process the data above. See the Unity Privacy Policy. The game is distributed through Google Play (Google Privacy Policy). We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Your profile and progress are kept until you delete them. Analytics data is kept in aggregated form for up to 2 years.

Deleting your data

In the game open Settings → Delete Data. This permanently deletes your account, progress and leaderboard entry from our servers and your phone. If you can no longer open the game, see how to request deletion by email.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkish KVKK) you may ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or delete it. Email veyroxgame@gmail.com and include your player name.

Children

Splat Siege is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored by Unity Gaming Services.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top.
