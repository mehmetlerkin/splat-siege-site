HomePrivacy Policy

Option 1: in the game (instant)

  1. Open Splat Siege.
  2. Tap the Settings tab (gear icon) at the bottom.
  3. Tap DELETE DATA, then DELETE to confirm.

Your account, player name, trophies, cards, chests, currencies, quest and Paint Pass progress and leaderboard entry are deleted from our servers immediately, and local data is removed from the phone.

Option 2: by email

If you cannot open the game, send an email to veyroxgame@gmail.com with the subject "Splat Siege data deletion" and your player name (and username, if you created an account). We delete the account and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Aggregated, anonymous analytics (for example "how many matches were played today") that cannot be linked back to you may be kept for up to 2 years.
